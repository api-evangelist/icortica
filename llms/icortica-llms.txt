# icortica

> icortica is a customer intelligence platform for dental laboratories. It connects a
> lab's existing systems and data, surfaces where revenue and retention risk actually
> sit, and gives sales representatives a specific set of actions to take for each
> account.

icortica, Inc — 89 Villa Street, Waltham, MA 02453, USA — +1 617-333-8306

## What the product does

- [How it works](https://www.icortica.com/how-it-works): the three-step setup —
  connecting existing systems, configuring to the lab's processes, and training — plus
  the Game Plan feature that recommends per-account actions.
- [Game Plan](https://www.icortica.com/game-plan): the AI feature in depth — the
  customer-data signals it reads and the prioritized, drafted actions it hands reps.
- [Integrations](https://www.icortica.com/integrations): the lab-management systems,
  accounting platforms and marketing tools icortica connects to.
- [ROI calculator](https://www.icortica.com/roi): estimates the revenue impact of
  improving onboarding, churn and customer counts.
